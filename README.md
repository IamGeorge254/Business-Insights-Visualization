# 📊 Business Sales Insights Dashboard

## 🧠 Problem Statement
In a competitive and rapidly changing market, businesses often struggle to gain real-time visibility into sales performance, customer value, and engagement patterns. The absence of actionable insights hinders effective decision-making and limits opportunities for targeted marketing, customer retention, and revenue optimization.

---

## 🎯 KPIs
- 📦 **Total Quantity by Category**  
  Total number of units sold per anonymized category.

- 💰 **Total Value by Category**  
  Monetary worth of sales across each category.

- 🥇 **Top-Performing Products**  
  Highest total value or volume sold.

- 🏢 **Top-Performing Businesses**  
  Businesses generating the highest revenue.

- 📈 **Sales Trends Over Time**  
  Monthly or time-series sales visualization.

- 👥 **Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis)**  
  - **Recency**: How recently a customer made a purchase.  
  - **Frequency**: How often they purchase.  
  - **Monetary**: How much they spend.
 
---

## 🔧 Steps in Your Process
- 🧼 **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Python)**  
  Handled missing values, duplicates, and corrected data types.

- 📊 **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**  
  Visualized trends, patterns, and relationships in data.

- ⚖️ **SMOTE for Class Balancing**  
  Applied SMOTE to handle class imbalance.

- ⏳ **Time Series Forecasting**  
  Forecasted trends using ARIMA or Prophet models.

- 📊 **Dashboard Design (Power BI)**  
  Designed dashboards for interactive storytelling.
